## Notes

- The spec names macOS platform features only where the owner's decision depends on them: the App
  Sandbox that selects the mode, and macOS 13 as the floor that makes a helper-free login item
  possible. Neither names an API.
- The command-line assumption, that the app's own executable reaches the in-app daemon from
  inside the sandbox, is the one assumption the plan must verify before relying on it.
